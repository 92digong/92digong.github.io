Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Travel Leisure Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Travel Leisure YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel. It requests only the permission needed to upload and manage videos on that channel.

What the app stores

The app stores one OAuth refresh token for the owner's channel as an encrypted secret in a private GitHub repository. It does not collect, store or share any data about other YouTube users or viewers.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to upload the channel's own videos, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions. Revoking it deletes the app's ability to use the stored token.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through its YouTube page, youtube.com/@travelleisure3156.
